Introduction

Welcome to Healthy Tantra ("we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information associated with your use of the Platform is collected, used, shared, and protected throughout your interaction with Healthy Tantra.

Healthy Tantra is a comprehensive healthcare and wellness platform that connects users with verified healthcare professionals, enables medicine and wellness product ordering, and provides access to yoga, wellness, and related services. We recognize the sensitive nature of health information and take our responsibility to protect it with utmost care.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Healthy Tantra Platform, including users, customers, wellness participants, dependents, caregivers, and visitors using our Platform and related services. Use of the Platform is also subject to Healthy Tantra's Terms of Use.

By accessing or using the Healthy Tantra Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the information handling practices described herein, subject to applicable law. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Platform.

App Store Data Safety and Privacy Nutrition Labels
Healthy Tantra maintains a Data Safety declaration on the Google Play Store and a Privacy Nutrition Label on the Apple App Store, which summarize the categories of data collected and how they are used. These declarations are consistent with this Privacy Policy. In the event of any conflict, this Privacy Policy shall prevail.

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws of India, without prejudice to any rights that may be available under mandatory laws applicable to a user.

1. Information You May Provide

1.1 Personal Information

When creating an account or using our services, you may voluntarily provide information including:

  • Name (first name, middle name, last name)
  • Phone Number (primary identifier for account creation and authentication)
  • Email Address (optional)
  • Date of Birth
  • Gender
  • Profile Photo (optional)
  • Username

1.2 Health and Medical Information

To receive healthcare and wellness services through the Platform, you may choose to provide, upload, share, or maintain health-related information including:

  • Blood Group
  • Weight and Height
  • Diet Preferences (dietary restrictions and preferences)
  • Lifestyle Information (such as smoking status or alcohol consumption)
  • Allergies, Intolerances, and Sensitivities
  • Current Medications and Medication History
  • Medical History (past medical conditions and treatments)
  • Surgical History (previous surgeries)
  • Symptoms and Health Concerns discussed during consultations
  • Prescriptions issued through the Platform
  • Diagnoses, treatment plans, and consultation notes provided by healthcare professionals
  • Medical Documents uploaded by you, including prescriptions, test reports, and medical certificates
  • Consultation Records and Follow-Up Information
  • Dependent or Family Profile Information, including medical information you choose to provide for family members or dependents managed through your account

Certain health-related information may constitute sensitive personal data under applicable laws and may be processed only for healthcare delivery, wellness services, operational purposes, safety, and legal compliance.

1.3 Financial Information

For payments, billing, and transaction management, information associated with purchases and payments may include:

  • Payment Transaction Details (amount, transaction ID, payment method, and transaction date)
  • Wallet Balance and Transaction History
  • Order and Purchase History
  • Billing Information (consultation charges, product costs, delivery fees)
  • Refund, Cancellation, and Settlement Records (where applicable)
Important: Healthy Tantra does not directly store or process your credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs, or banking credentials. Payment-related processing is handled securely by Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS compliant.

1.4 Location Information

To enable deliveries and related services, you may provide:

  • Delivery Addresses (street address, locality, city, state, pincode)
  • Receiver Name and Phone Number (for delivery purposes)

Healthy Tantra does not continuously track or monitor your real-time GPS location unless explicitly permitted by you for a specific feature or service.

1.5 Communications

Communications and interactions through the Platform may include:

  • Chat Messages exchanged with healthcare professionals during consultations
  • Support Ticket Messages submitted to customer support
  • File Attachments shared in chats or support requests
  • Consultation Communications, including chat, audio, video, or follow-up interactions
  • Video/Audio Call Metadata (such as call duration, timestamps, and call type)

Consultation audio, video, chat, or communication records may be recorded, monitored, retained, reviewed, or disclosed where permitted by applicable law for quality assurance, training, safety, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, regulatory compliance, or legal purposes. Where required by law, appropriate notice and consent mechanisms will be provided before any recording takes place.

1.6 Device and Technical Information

  • Device Identifiers (such as FCM token for push notifications)
  • Device Type and Platform Information (Android/iOS version)
  • App Version
  • IP Address
  • Browser Type and Operating System Information
  • Session Logs and Authentication Records
  • Technical Logs related to app crashes, errors, and API activity
  • Analytics and Performance Data (feature usage, crash diagnostics, performance metrics)

1.6A Cookies and Similar Technologies

Healthy Tantra's website and web-based services may use cookies, pixels, local storage, SDKs, and similar technologies to support authentication, security, performance monitoring, analytics, user preferences, and service functionality. Users may control certain cookie settings through their browser or device settings; however, disabling certain technologies may affect Platform functionality.

Third-Party Websites and Services

The Platform may contain links to third-party websites, applications, services, payment providers, pharmacies, laboratories, healthcare providers, or external resources. Healthy Tantra is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party services.

1.7 Usage Information

  • Consultation History (past, ongoing, and scheduled appointments)
  • Product Browsing and Search History
  • Shopping Cart and Wishlist Activity
  • Order History and Delivery Status
  • Ratings and Reviews submitted for healthcare professionals or products
  • Referral Activity (if you refer others or were referred)
  • App Interaction Data, including screens viewed, feature usage, and notification interactions

1.8 How and When We Obtain Your Consent

Healthy Tantra obtains your consent at appropriate points during your use of the Platform. You will never be required to provide sensitive personal or health information without being given a clear opportunity to consent.

Account Creation

When you create an account, you are presented with this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use. By completing account registration, you acknowledge how your personal information will be collected, used, and shared.

Health and Medical Information

Before you provide, upload, or share health and medical information, you will be given a clear opportunity to review how that information will be used. Providing health information is voluntary; however, certain information may be necessary to receive specific services.

Consultation Recording

Where applicable law requires consent before a consultation record is retained or monitored, you will be notified and given the opportunity to consent before the consultation begins. You may withdraw consent for non-essential recording at any time, subject to the limitations described in Section 6.4.

Push Notifications

Your device operating system (Android or iOS) will prompt you to grant permission for push notifications when you first use the Platform. You may manage or withdraw notification permissions at any time through your device settings.

Marketing and Promotional Communications

Promotional and marketing communications via email, SMS, or WhatsApp are sent only where you have opted in or where permitted under applicable law. You may opt out at any time through unsubscribe links, account settings, or by contacting care@healthytantra.com.

Dependent Profiles

When creating a profile for a dependent or family member, the parent or legal guardian provides consent on behalf of the dependent and is responsible for ensuring that consent is appropriate and given in accordance with applicable law.

Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw consent at any time as described in Section 6.4. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

3. How Information May Be Shared

Healthy Tantra respects user privacy and limits information sharing to the circumstances described below.

3.1 With Healthcare Professionals

When healthcare services are requested, relevant information may be made available to the selected healthcare professional, including:

  • Name, age, gender, and contact information
  • Medical history, surgical history, and current symptoms
  • Weight, height, blood group, and diet preferences
  • Lifestyle information voluntarily shared by users
  • Previous prescriptions and medical documents uploaded by users
  • Consultation chats, notes, and follow-up information

Healthcare professionals are independent practitioners. Healthy Tantra does not practice medicine, provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or control the professional judgment of healthcare professionals. Healthcare professionals are expected to maintain confidentiality in accordance with applicable professional, ethical, and legal standards.

3.2 With Service Providers

Healthy Tantra works with trusted third-party service providers including:

  • ZEGOCLOUD (video and audio consultation infrastructure)
  • Razorpay (payment processing)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)

These service providers are contractually required to maintain security and confidentiality measures equivalent to those described in this Privacy Policy, and may not use any data shared with them for their own independent purposes.

3.3 With User Authorization or Request

Information may be shared when explicitly requested or authorized by the user, including sharing prescriptions, medical documents, or consultation details with healthcare professionals or caregivers specified by the user.

Healthy Tantra does not sell personal information or medical information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3.4 Legal Compliance and Protection

Information may be disclosed to courts, regulatory authorities, law enforcement agencies, governmental bodies, auditors, or other authorized parties where required by law, legal process, regulatory obligation, investigation, or enforcement action.

3.5 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, information associated with the Platform may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and appropriate data protection safeguards.

4. Data Security

Reasonable and industry-standard security measures are implemented to help protect information associated with the Platform.

Security Incidents: If Healthy Tantra becomes aware of a security incident or personal data breach that requires notification under applicable law, reasonable steps will be taken to investigate, contain, mitigate, and provide required notifications to affected individuals, regulators, or authorities.

4.1 Technical Security Measures

  • HTTPS (SSL/TLS) encryption for data transmission
  • Secure communication protocols for real-time messaging and consultations
  • Industry-standard protection measures for sensitive information
  • OTP-based authentication and authenticated API access mechanisms
  • PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through trusted payment providers

Health Data and Device Storage

Healthy Tantra does not store personal health information or medical records in Apple iCloud or any Apple-managed cloud storage. Health and medical data is stored on Healthy Tantra's secured servers. On iOS devices, any locally cached app data is protected by the device's built-in encryption and is accessible only while the device is unlocked. Users are advised to enable device passcode or biometric authentication to protect locally stored app data.

4.2 Organizational Security Measures

  • Restricted access to authorized personnel only
  • Confidentiality obligations for employees, contractors, and partners
  • Security monitoring and periodic reviews
  • Incident response procedures for potential security events

4.3 User Security Best Practices

Users are encouraged to:

  • Keep phone numbers and OTPs confidential
  • Avoid sharing accounts with others
  • Log out from shared or public devices
  • Report suspicious activity immediately through support channels

While reasonable efforts are made to protect information, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We collect and retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected. We do not retain personal data longer than is required for these purposes.

6.1 How Long We Retain Your Data

Data TypeRetention Period
Account and profile informationDuration of active account + 3 years after account deletion
Health and medical records7 years from the date of last consultation or interaction
Consultation records and prescriptions7 years from the date of issue
Payment and transaction records8 years from the date of transaction (as required under Indian tax and accounting laws)
Support and communication records3 years from the date of last interaction
Device identifiers and technical logs90 days from the date of collection
Marketing and notification preferencesUntil the user opts out or the account is deleted

Following the expiration of the applicable retention period, personal data is securely deleted, anonymized, aggregated, or archived in accordance with applicable law. Where mandatory retention applies beyond your deletion request, you will be informed of the specific data retained, the reason, and the applicable retention period.

6.2 How to Delete Your Data

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. Healthy Tantra supports account and data deletion directly within the app, as well as through email.

In-App Deletion (Primary Method)

  • Healthy Tantra app:
    Profile → Edit Profile → Delete icon (bin icon, top right corner)
  • Healthy Tantra Provider app:
    Profile → Request for Delete Account

Initiating deletion within the app will submit a verified request to permanently delete your account record and all associated personal data that Healthy Tantra is not legally required to retain.

Email Deletion Request

  • Email: care@healthytantra.com or grievance@healthytantra.com
  • Subject line: "Data Deletion Request"
  • Please include your registered phone number or account details.
Important: Simply uninstalling the app from your device does not delete your account or your personal data from Healthy Tantra's servers. You must submit a deletion request through the app or by email as described above.

6.3 Deletion Processing Timeline

Upon receiving a deletion request (in-app or via email):

  • Account access will be immediately disabled.
  • Your data will enter a 30-day processing period during which deletion is finalized across all active systems.
  • Within 30 days of receiving your request, all personal data — including profile details, medical records, uploaded documents, and communication logs — will be permanently deleted from active databases, except data required to be retained under mandatory legal or regulatory requirements.

6.4 Legal Exceptions to Deletion

Certain data may be retained even after a deletion request where retention is required by law, regulation, or legitimate legal purposes, including:

  • Medical and consultation records retained to comply with legal obligations applicable to healthcare documentation (retained for up to 7 years)
  • Financial and transaction records retained to comply with tax, accounting, and audit laws (retained for up to 8 years)
  • Data necessary to resolve active disputes, enforce legal agreements, or respond to regulatory inquiries
  • Data necessary for fraud prevention, security monitoring, or investigating potential terms violations

Where data is retained under a legal exception, it will be isolated, access-restricted, and processed solely for the specific legal purpose for which it was retained.
