How Online Wellness Consultations Work

Healthy Tantra simplifies online wellness consultations by helping users connect with verified wellness professionals in a safe and structured environment.

Step 1 — Choose Your Wellness Need

Users can explore different wellness categories including Ayurveda, Homeopathy, Yoga, mental wellness, nutrition guidance, and lifestyle wellness support.

Step 2 — Find Verified Professionals

Only verified wellness professionals are listed on the platform. Every professional undergoes identity verification and ethical review.

Step 3 — Book a Consultation

Users can schedule online consultations based on preferred time slots and wellness requirements.

Step 4 — Secure Consultation Experience

Consultations are conducted in a secure and privacy-focused environment. Healthy Tantra prioritizes user confidentiality and safe communication.
