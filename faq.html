What is Healthy Tantra?

Healthy Tantra is an online doctor consultation and healthcare app. It brings together verified doctors and healthcare professionals across Allopathy, Ayurveda, Homeopathy, and Unani, so you can consult, book appointments, and order medicines — all in one place, anywhere in India.

The name reflects that idea. "Tantra" is a Sanskrit word for system or framework — as in pachan tantra, the digestive system. Healthy Tantra brings that same thinking to your health: instead of being limited to one way of healing, you get a complete system, built around live consultations, scheduled appointments, and medicines, all in one app.

Every doctor on Healthy Tantra is verified, so you can consult with confidence and pay only for the minutes you actually use.

How is Healthy Tantra different from other online consultation apps?
Healthy Tantra is an online doctor consultation platform that lets you pay by the minute, so you only pay for the time you actually use. You can consult an Allopathy, Ayurveda, Homeopathy, or Unani doctor, and choose based on their experience, system of medicine, consultation fee, and availability. You can also book a scheduled appointment with your preferred doctor. Beyond consultations, Healthy Tantra also offers a medicine and wellness marketplace, so consultations, appointments, medicines, and more are all available in one place.
