Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Healthy Tantra, how the platform works, and how we ensure safe and responsible wellness guidance.

Is Healthy Tantra a medical service or hospital?
No. Healthy Tantra is a wellness guidance platform focused on lifestyle support, stress management, and holistic well-being. It does not provide emergency medical services or medical diagnosis.
What is the purpose of Healthy Tantra?
Healthy Tantra connects users with verified wellness professionals who provide structured guidance for improving lifestyle balance, emotional well-being, and daily habits.
Are wellness professionals verified?
Yes. All professionals are verified through an internal process that includes identity validation and review of relevant wellness background before they are allowed to offer consultations.
What happens during a consultation?
Users can connect with a verified wellness professional to discuss lifestyle concerns, stress patterns, sleep habits, and general well-being guidance in a private and structured session.
Is my personal data and consultation private?
Yes. All personal information and consultation details are handled with strict confidentiality and are not shared without user consent.
Can Healthy Tantra replace a doctor or emergency care?
No. Healthy Tantra is not a replacement for medical treatment, diagnosis, or emergency healthcare services. Users should seek appropriate medical attention when required.
Who should use Healthy Tantra?
Anyone looking for structured wellness guidance, lifestyle improvement, stress management support, and holistic well-being insights can use the platform.
How do I book a consultation?
Users can browse verified professionals on the platform, select a suitable expert, and book a consultation at a convenient time slot through the app or website.
