Pay Only for the Minutes you consult

Most online consultations charge one flat fee, whether your visit takes 3 minutes or 30. On Healthy Tantra you pay only for the time you actually spend with your doctor. There are no hidden charges, and the rate is shown upfront before you connect.

That makes it easier to start a quick follow-up or ask a short question without paying for a full session.

Frequently asked questions

Can I consult a doctor online on Healthy Tantra?
Yes. Download the Healthy Tantra app from the App Store or Google Play, choose a verified doctor, and start a live consultation or book an appointment for later.
Do I need to register before I consult a doctor?
You can browse doctors and start a consultation without a lengthy sign-up. Open the app, pick a doctor and connect.
Is online doctor consultation free on Healthy Tantra, and how much does it cost?
Consultations are not free. Healthy Tantra uses Pay Only for the Minutes pricing, so you pay only for the time you actually spend with the doctor. The rate is shown upfront before you connect, with no hidden charges.
Can I book an online doctor appointment?
Yes. In the Healthy Tantra app you can connect with a doctor live, or schedule an appointment with your preferred doctor at a time that works for you.
Can I consult a doctor on the Healthy Tantra website?
No. For security and a better experience, consultations and orders are available only on the Healthy Tantra app for Android and iPhone.
Which types of doctors can I consult?
You can consult verified doctors and practitioners in Allopathy, Ayurveda, Homeopathy and Unani, along with Diet and Nutrition and Mental Health professionals.
Is my online consultation private and secure?
Yes. Consultations take place inside the app, which is designed to keep your conversation with the doctor secure and private. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I order medicines after my consultation?
Yes. You can order prescribed medicines and browse Ayurveda, Homeopathy and Unani products and wellness essentials in the same app.
Is Healthy Tantra suitable for emergencies?
No. Healthy Tantra is not intended for emergency medical situations. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call your local emergency number immediately.
