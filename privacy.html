Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, such as when you create an account, book a consultation, or contact support. This may include basic personal details like name, email address, and platform usage data.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve our wellness guidance services, manage consultations, ensure platform safety, and communicate important updates.

Wellness Platform Scope

Healthy Tantra is a wellness guidance platform that connects users with verified wellness professionals for lifestyle, stress management, and holistic well-being support.

It does not provide emergency medical services, diagnosis, or treatment and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical care.

Consultation Data

Information shared during consultations is treated as confidential and used only to facilitate wellness guidance within the platform. We take reasonable measures to ensure privacy and security.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration.

Data Retention

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for using the platform appropriately and seeking qualified medical attention in case of emergencies or serious health conditions.

Third-Party Services

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of changes.
